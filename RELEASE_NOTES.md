### ARM64 downloads

Windows and Linux now have ARM64 builds. The app also includes x64 builds for those platforms and Apple Silicon and Intel ZIPs for macOS.

Make the Linux AppImage executable before launching. It can run with `--appimage-extract-and-run` on systems without FUSE. macOS builds require macOS 14 or newer and are ad hoc signed. [Apple’s first-launch instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445)

Opening an original and copied `.avc` together in Media Composer still needs a live test.
